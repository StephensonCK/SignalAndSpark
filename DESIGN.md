# Signal & Spark: printed 1980s poster

User reference: Retro 80s Login Page UI Design. The target is a tactile music poster, with brush lettering, condensed print type, aged stock, faded raspberry ink, midnight blue and restrained cyan. Existing album art and music remain the content anchors.

Design variance 7, motion intensity 2, visual density 6. Native CSS over the existing static site; no framework migration. poster.css owns this art direction, layered over the functional responsive styles.

Self-hosted Permanent Marker supplies brush lettering. Barlow Condensed regular and semibold supply the chrome display title, navigation, song names and body. License files are in assets/fonts. This replaces reliance on platform fonts for the visible identity.

Composition: a road-scene poster on the left and taped listening sleeve on the right. Mobile stacks these. The collection uses individual printed covers against the dark poster, not uniform beige cards. Light paper is a physical object within the dark theme. Print grain is a small local raster overlay with no interaction interception.

Player and lyrics keep native audio controls, visible Play/Pause text, named dialog and song-specific labels. Lyrics scroll independently of the close button and player. Escape restores focus, and player close returns focus to its originating control. Reduced motion is supported. The site stays local and unpublished.

Latest direction: Rock Salt replaces Permanent Marker for handwritten lettering. The outer canvas is midnight navy, with stronger hot pink and cyan accents and tapered swoosh underlines beneath selected headings. Paper remains limited to the physical listening sleeve and note. Rock Salt is self-hosted with its license.

Texture is restricted to the dark site background. Artwork, lettering, controls, and paper surfaces have no grain overlay.

## Brand cleanup
The site uses midnight surfaces, straight cover edges, restrained cyan borders, and pink swooshes. Paper, tape, tilted cards, and the rounded outer frame are retired. Rock Salt remains the heading font by Courtney's preference. Texture stays on the background only. Navigation lives below the hero.

## Physical detail refinement
Keep the dark palette and unframed page, with a slight featured-release tilt and one pink tape tab. Album covers have thin chrome-light sleeve edges and subtle alternating angles. Story copy sits on a dark blue liner-notes panel. No texture overlays on content.

## White paper iteration
Featured release and story liner notes use warm white (#f3f1ec) with dark ink, pink accents, inset borders, slight angles, and shadows. Keep the midnight surroundings and dark lyrics dialog. No grain on paper or artwork.
